Welcome to DHIS2 DataBridge ⚡

A web-based analytics tool for querying, comparing, cleaning, and drawing insights from DHIS2 health data — built by Wadson Chirwa, Environmental Health Technician & Deputy IDSR Coordinator, Zomba District Health Office, Malawi. No installation required.

Periods
10

⚠️ This dataset includes intentional quality issues (missing values, outliers) so you can explore the Data Quality tab.

📋 Paste Your Data

Supported formats: CSV, TSV, or semicolon-delimited. First row should be headers. Expected columns: district / org_unit, period, indicator, value (column order flexible).

Query & Explore

Use natural language or manual filters to query your data. Hold Ctrl/Cmd to multi-select.
